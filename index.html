🌀 Endless Labyrinth

Guide your pulsing orb from the green start pad to the spinning diamond portal exit. As soon as you step into the portal, the stopwatch records your lap and warps you into the next procedural labyrinth!

Controls

  • Move W A S D or Arrow Keys
  • 5-Second Hint H
  • Toggle Camera Space or C
  • Switch Theme T
  • Toggle Sound M
  • Restart Maze R

5-Second Fading Hint

Clicking Hint displays the optimal path at full brightness for 3.5 seconds, then smoothly fades out over the last 1.5 seconds. The Hint button locks with a live countdown during the hint.

Clock Lap Splits

Every time you conquer a maze, your lap time and step count are logged in the side panel just like a stopwatch lap tracker, highlighting your fastest runs!
